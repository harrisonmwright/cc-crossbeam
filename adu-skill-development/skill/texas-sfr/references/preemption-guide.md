# Texas State Preemption — Quick Reference for Plan Checkers

Use this table during Phase 3 state law verification. For each finding that cites a local LDC provision, check here first: if the local rule is more restrictive than the state floor, the local rule is preempted and the correction cannot be issued.

**Key principle:** State law is a floor, not a ceiling. Cities can be more permissive. They cannot be more restrictive.

---

## SB 15 Preemption Table (Tex. Local Gov't Code §§211.051–211.058)

*Applies to qualifying municipalities (pop. >150,000 in a county >300,000). Effective September 1, 2025.*

### All Residential Lots (New Plats)

| Topic | State Floor (Max restriction city may impose) | Local Rule Preempted If... | Action |
|-------|----------------------------------------------|---------------------------|--------|
| Minimum lot size | 3,000 sq ft | City requires >3,000 sq ft minimum | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.054 |
| Minimum lot width | 30 ft | City requires >30 ft minimum | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.054 |
| Minimum lot depth | 75 ft | City requires >75 ft minimum | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.054 |
| Density | Must allow SFH on ≥3,000 sq ft lot | City density rule prevents SFH on a conforming lot | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.053 |

### Small Lots Only (≤4,000 sq ft)

| Topic | State Floor | Local Rule Preempted If... | Action |
|-------|-------------|---------------------------|--------|
| Front setback | Max 15 ft | Local requires >15 ft | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(1)(A) |
| Rear setback | Max 10 ft | Local requires >10 ft | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(1)(B) |
| Side setback | Max 5 ft | Local requires >5 ft | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(1)(C) |
| Covered parking | **Cannot require** | Local requires covered parking | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(2) |
| Parking spaces | Max 1 per unit | Local requires ≥2 per unit | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(3) |
| Off-site parking | **Cannot require** | Local requires off-site parking | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(4) |
| Open space / permeable surface | Max 30% required | Local requires >30% permeable | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(5) |
| Stories | Cannot require fewer than 3 full stories | Local caps at <3 stories for small lots | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(6) |
| Maximum building bulk | **Cannot restrict** | Local imposes bulk standard | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(7) |
| Wall articulation | **Cannot require** | Local requires articulation | Do not issue correction; cite SB 15 §211.055(a)(8) |
| Overlay district restrictions inconsistent with above | **Prohibited** | Overlay imposes more restrictive rules | Flag and cite SB 15 §211.055(b) |

### What SB 15 Does NOT Preempt (Cities may still require)
- Shared driveway access with adjacent lots
- Permitting fees (same as standard SFR)
- Impact fees under Ch. 395
- Setbacks required by environmental, erosion, or waterway protection
- Aquifer protection measures in recharge zones

## SB 840 Preemption Table (Tex. Local Gov't Code, Chapter 218)

*Applies to new development and conversions filed on or after September 1, 2025 in commercially-zoned areas.*

| Topic | State Floor | Local Rule Preempted If... | Action |
|-------|-------------|---------------------------|--------|
| By-right allowance for multifamily/mixed-use | Must allow by right in any commercial zone | City requires CUP, SUP, or rezoning for multifamily in commercial zone | Do not issue correction; cite SB 840 §218.101 |
| Density cap | Max of: highest local residential density OR 36 units/acre | Local cap is below 36 units/acre AND below city's highest residential density | Do not issue correction; cite SB 840 §218.102(a)(2) |
| Height limit | Max of: commercial height on same site OR 45 ft | Local limit is below 45 ft AND below the commercial height allowed | Do not issue correction; cite SB 840 §218.102(a)(1)(B)(ii) |
| Setback/buffer | Min of: commercial setback on same site OR 25 ft | Local requires setback >25 ft AND >commercial setback | Do not issue correction; cite SB 840 §218.102(a)(4) |
| Parking | Max 1 space per unit | Local requires >1 per unit for multifamily | Do not issue correction; cite SB 840 §218.102(a)(5)(A) |
| Multilevel parking structure | **Cannot require** | Local requires parking garage | Do not issue correction; cite SB 840 §218.102(a)(5)(B) |
| FAR restriction | **Cannot restrict FAR** | Local imposes FAR limit on multifamily | Do not issue correction; cite SB 840 §218.102(a)(3) |
| Nonresidential component requirement | **Cannot require** in non-mixed-use zone | Local requires retail on ground floor in a non-mixed-use zone | Do not issue correction; cite SB 840 §218.102(a)(6) |

### Conversion of Commercial Buildings to Residential

| Topic | What SB 840 Prohibits |
|-------|----------------------|
| Traffic impact analysis | Cannot require for conversion permits |
| Traffic mitigation fees | Cannot require |
| Additional parking beyond existing | Cannot require |
| Utility extensions beyond building need | Cannot require |
| Exterior design beyond IBC minimums | Cannot require |
| Interior space standards beyond IBC | Cannot require |
| Impact fees (if not previously applicable) | Cannot impose |

**SB 840 not applicable to:** Heavy industrial zones, land within 1,000 ft of heavy industrial, land within 3,000 ft of airport or military base, clear zones.

## HB 24 — Plan Review Note

HB 24 governs how cities **process zoning changes** (legislative procedures, protest rights, notice requirements). It does **not** create development standards. **Do not cite HB 24 in a plan correction letter.** It is background context only.

## Building Code Preemption

| Topic | State Floor | Local Rule Preempted If... | Action |
|-------|-------------|---------------------------|--------|
| IRC edition | IRC 2021 (Tex. Gov't Code §214.212) | City adopts an edition older than 2021 | State baseline governs; use IRC 2021 |
| Texas Energy Code | IECC 2021 | City adopts older IECC | State baseline governs; use IECC 2021 |
| NEC edition | NEC 2020 | City adopts older NEC | State baseline governs |
| City may NOT adopt less stringent codes | — | City ordinance reduces below state minimum | State floor governs |

## Decision Tree for State Preemption Check

```
Finding cites a local LDC provision?
    │
    ▼
Is the project a new plat (≥5 acres, SFR, no existing recorded plat)?
    │
    ├─ YES → Apply SB 15 new-plat rules (lot size, width, depth)
    │
    └─ NO → Is the lot ≤4,000 sq ft?
                │
                ├─ YES → Apply SB 15 small-lot rules (setbacks, parking, bulk)
                │
                └─ NO → Is the project in a commercial zone?
                            │
                            ├─ YES → Apply SB 840 (by-right MF, height, density, FAR)
                            │
                            └─ NO → No SB 15/840 preemption; cite local LDC rule if valid
```

## Common Errors to Avoid

| Error | Correct Approach |
|-------|-----------------|
| Issuing correction for covered parking on a small lot | Mark PASS; note SB 15 §211.055(a)(2) preempts the local requirement |
| Citing lot width >30 ft as a correction when SB 15 applies | Mark PASS; state floor is 30 ft maximum minimum |
| Applying FAR restriction to a multifamily project in commercial zone | Mark PASS; SB 840 §218.102(a)(3) prohibits FAR restrictions |
| Citing the local rule when city is more permissive than state | Correct — city more permissive governs; this is NOT preemption |
| Applying SB 15 to a city that doesn't qualify by population | Verify qualifying population before applying SB 15 preemption |
| Applying SB 15 to permits filed before Sept 1, 2025 | SB 15 does not apply retroactively |
