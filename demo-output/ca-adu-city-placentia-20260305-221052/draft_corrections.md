### Info B — Development Impact Fee (ADU >750 sq ft)
**Code:** Government Code § 66333.1 / Placentia Development Impact Fee Schedule

The proposed ADU is 850 sq ft. California law exempts ADUs ≤750 sq ft from development impact fees. For ADUs between 751–1,000 sq ft, a proportional impact fee applies. The applicant should contact the City's Finance Department for the current fee schedule and to obtain a fee estimate prior to permit issuance.

### Info C — Water Service: Golden State Water Company Coordination
**Utility Provider:** Golden State Water Company (GSWC)

Water service in Placentia is provided by Golden State Water Company (GSWC), a private water utility. The City of Placentia does not process water utility connections. Any new water service lateral, meter, or service upgrade for the ADU must be coordinated directly with GSWC. The applicant should contact GSWC early in the construction process to confirm service availability and any required upgrades.
